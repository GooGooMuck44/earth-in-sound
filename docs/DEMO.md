## Seven-slide fallback

1. **Earth in Sound.** Hear Earth change. Team name and members.
2. **Problem.** Climate charts are easy to skip and hard or impossible for some people to use.
3. **Idea.** NASA data becomes sound, with a chart and values that move with it.
4. **Four NASA sources, six views.** Planet, Poles, My Place, Compare, Events, Scan; one finding each.
5. **Honest science.** Shared scales, baselines, units, missing data, the Data panel and CSV.
6. **Built for real rooms.** Offline, keyboard, screen-reader labels, no account.
7. **Next.** Test with listeners and teachers, add Bangla, classroom activities. Links and credits.

          About

          Earth in Sound turns NASA Earth data into sound you can explore. Higher notes mean higher values. The chart, the readout and the Data panel show the same numbers, so you can use it with or without sound.

          Views

          Planet
          Global temperature each year, compared with 1951–1980.
          Regions
          Three latitude bands, one in the left ear, one in the middle, one in the right: poles and tropics, hemispheres, or northern and southern bands.
          My Place
          A city month by month: temperature, rain and sunlight.
          Compare
          Two cities, one in each ear, on one shared temperature scale.
          Events
          Natural events NASA tracked over the last 60 days.
          Scan
          A line sweeps a NASA satellite map from west to east.
